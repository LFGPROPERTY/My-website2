Our story

Creating Value That Endures

Over more than a decade, LFG has built a reputation for delivering premium residential, commercial and hospitality assets across Sydney and beyond.

Through an integrated model encompassing development, construction oversight, investment and hotel operations, LFG manages every stage of an asset's lifecycle—not simply its delivery. This operator-led perspective, underpinned by disciplined capital management, ensures that every project remains focused on enduring value rather than a single completion date.

Our services

“To develop and operate assets with the discipline of an owner — delivering quality that holds its value long after completion.”

What sets us apart

A practical partner across the asset lifecycle

Three principles guide every project LFG takes on, from acquisition through to day-to-day operation.

01

Integrated delivery

Development, investment, construction management and hotel operations are held within one group — so decisions are never made in isolation.

02

Operator mindset

We stay focused on how an asset performs after completion, not only on how it is delivered — the same discipline whether it's an apartment block or a hotel.

03

Long-term ownership

Every decision is weighed against a long horizon: quality, resilience and enduring value over short-term speed.

What guides us

Quality
Every division works to the same standard, from design coordination through to construction and finished handover — consistency is non-negotiable.
Discipline
Commercial assessment and capital discipline sit behind every acquisition and design decision, keeping each project focused on long-term performance.
Compliance & sustainability
Governance and regulatory compliance are built into project planning from the outset, alongside a responsible approach to project outcomes.
Community
We engage collaboratively with authorities, partners and the communities our developments become part of, well beyond completion.
