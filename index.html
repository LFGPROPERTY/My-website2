Our story

Discipline as our blueprint

Over more than a decade, LFG has built a reputation for delivering premium residential, commercial and hospitality assets across Sydney and beyond. The portfolio spans luxury residential development on Sydney's North Shore, commercial holdings in Chatswood, and hotel assets stretching from Tasmania to Tropical North Queensland.

Through an integrated model covering development, construction oversight, investment and hotel operations, LFG manages every stage of an asset's life — not just its delivery. That operator's perspective, combined with disciplined capital management, is what keeps every project focused on enduring value rather than a single completion date.

What sets us apart

A practical partner across the asset lifecycle

Three principles guide every project LFG takes on, from acquisition through to day-to-day operation.

01

Integrated delivery

Development, investment, construction management and hotel operations are held within one group — so decisions are never made in isolation.

02

Operator mindset

We stay focused on how an asset performs after completion, not only on how it is delivered — the same discipline whether it's an apartment block or a hotel.

03

Long-term ownership

Every decision is weighed against a long horizon: quality, resilience and enduring value over short-term speed.

What guides us

Quality
Every division works to the same standard, from design coordination through to construction and finished handover — consistency is non-negotiable.
Discipline

Compliance & sustainability
Governance and regulatory compliance are built into project planning from the outset, alongside a responsible approach to project outcomes.
Community

Hospitality

LFG's hospitality arm oversees end-to-end hotel management on behalf of owners and investors — building high-performing teams and delivering strong operational outcomes across every property.
